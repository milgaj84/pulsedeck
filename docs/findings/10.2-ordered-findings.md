## Ordering Methodology

### HIGH Priority — Ordered by: dependency graph (topological sort), then impact
- Each HIGH finding annotated with execution dependencies
- Dependency graph verified acyclic (DAG property holds)

### MEDIUM Priority — Ordered by: impact score = modules_affected × frequency × bug_risk_factor
- `modules_affected`: count of source files touched by the finding or its fix
- `frequency`: how often the pattern repeats (1 = single instance, N = occurrences)
- `bug_risk_factor`: 1.0 (low), 1.5 (moderate), 2.0 (high risk of future bugs)

### LOW Priority — Ordered by: effort (smallest first = quick wins)
- Effort estimated as relative t-shirt size: XS (minutes), S (< 1 hour), M (1-3 hours)
